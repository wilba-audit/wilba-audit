# How to Make "The Claude Screen-Recording" Content
## The Albert-style videos (icon pops up, prompt types, skill fires). It's screen-recording + polish — not AI.

**The big secret:** those slick videos are **a real screen recording** of Claude doing the thing, run through a **polish tool that auto-zooms and smooths the cursor**, with a **talking head** cut over it. That's it. The "magic" is one app.

---

## The toolstack

### 1. Screen recording (the core)
- **Screen Studio** (Mac, ~$20/mo) — ⭐ **this is the secret weapon.** You just record your screen; it *automatically* adds the smooth zoom-ins to your cursor, the polished cursor, rounded corners, and a nice background. This one tool is 80% of "why does theirs look so good." Most of these creators use it.
- Free alternatives: **QuickTime** (Mac) or the built-in Windows/phone screen recorder → then polish in the editor below.
- **Phone:** native screen record works if you're using the Claude app on mobile.

### 2. The editor (assemble + captions + your face)
- **CapCut** (free, desktop + mobile) — ⭐ easiest. Add your talking-head in a corner, auto-captions, music, the screen-record as the main visual, speed-ramps.
- Alternatives: **Descript** (edit video by editing text — great for non-technical), **Premiere** (pro).

### 3. Your face
- Just your phone. Film the hook + key lines. Drop it in a corner (picture-in-picture) or intercut with the screen-record.

### 4. B-roll, thumbnails, intro cards
- **Higgsfield / Artlist** — for the polish shots *around* the screen-record (a villa at dusk, an intro card, the thumbnail). NOT for the screen part.

---

## The exact workflow (one video)
1. **Record your screen** in Screen Studio: open Claude Code, type the prompt / run the skill, let the result appear. Do it cleanly once. (Type the prompt deliberately — that's the "prompt typing" shot.)
2. **Film your face** on your phone: the hook ("here's an AI skill that books guests at 2am") + the payoff line.
3. **Drop both into CapCut:** screen-record as the main visual, your face in the corner or intercut, auto-captions on, a beat of music.
4. **Speed-ramp** the boring bits (the AI thinking) so it's snappy.
5. **Cover/thumbnail** from Higgsfield/Artlist in your brand.
6. Export 9:16 → post.

**Total once you've got the tools: ~20–30 min a video.** Faster as you go.

## How it maps to your posts
Every `/skill-drop` gives you the *script + the skill to show on screen*. So your recording is: **"here's the prompt" (type it) → "here's what it does" (result appears) → "comment [WORD] for the skill."** The screen-record IS your proof and your authority. Real screen + your face + captions = the whole look.

## What to buy / install first
- **Screen Studio** (if you're on Mac) — the single biggest upgrade to how your content looks.
- **CapCut** (free) — your editor.
- That's it. You already have the phone, Higgsfield, and Artlist.
