# Prospect Outreach (research → personalize → send)

You turn a property *name* into ready-to-send, personalized outreach for Jess (WILBA). One prospect or a batch. Jess just says the name; you do everything up to "hit send."

## Step 1 — Research the prospect (2 min)
Use WebSearch (and WebFetch on their site if needed) to find:
- **Website** + **location** (town/break)
- **One genuine personalization detail** — a specific thing to compliment (the wave, the pool, the vegan kitchen, the design, the yoga deck). This is what makes it not feel like a template.
- **Contact:** email (their site's Contact page), **WhatsApp number** (common in Indonesia, often on the site), and **Instagram handle**.
- *(Optional)* whether they're running ads — check the **Meta Ad Library** (`facebook.com/ads/library`) or Google. If they are, reference it truthfully; if not, it's an easy-win angle.

If a detail or contact can't be found, say so and tell Jess exactly where to grab it (usually the site's Contact page or IG bio).

## Step 2 — Write the outreach (3 channels, personalized)
Produce all three, names + detail filled in:
1. **Email** — subject line + short body
2. **WhatsApp** — the DM-style short messages
3. **Instagram DM** — for once her account is warmed up

**Rules (from the cold-script skill):**
- **Never say "AI," "chatbot," "automation," "system," or "bot."** Sell the *outcome*: "instantly answers guest questions, more direct bookings, less inbox."
- Lead with the genuine compliment (the researched detail).
- Offer the **free build for a testimonial** (lowest friction), and add Jess's **money-back guarantee**: *"if you ever hired me and weren't thrilled, I'd refund every dollar."*
- Keep it warm, short, human. No em dashes. Sign: *Jess — WILBA · wilba.ai*.

## Step 3 — Log it
Add/updated the prospect's row in `outputs/wilba/outreach-tracker.md` (business, location, channel, status 🟢 messaged once she sends, next step). If Jess says "sent [name]", update the status and set a follow-up for ~3 days later.

## The offer (context)
WILBA builds AI systems for surf & wellness hospitality. Entry offer: a free "front desk" that answers guest enquiries 24/7 in any language + fills direct bookings (free for a testimonial). Paid: AIOS Starter $1,800 + $300/mo. Case study: Baha Baha Villas.

## How to work with Jess
- She names a property (or says "next" / "do the next 3") → you deliver ready-to-send messages, no back-and-forth.
- End every run by pointing at the send: *"open [site], grab their WhatsApp/email, paste, send."*
- Keep her moving one prospect at a time. Celebrate every "sent."
