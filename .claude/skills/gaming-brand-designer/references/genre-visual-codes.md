# Genre visual codes

These are moods to *evoke*. Never copy the publisher's logo, font or characters.

| Genre / game world | Palette | Lettering attitude | Motifs | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open-world crime (GTA-like) | Sunset pink, purple, orange, teal on black | Heavy condensed or italic, thick black outline, hard offset shadow, poster/sticker energy | Palms, skylines, neon grids, wanted-level stars (generic), cars, cash, stamps | Pricedown font, the "VI" numeral logo, Rockstar key-art style portraits |
| Battle royale / cartoon shooter (Fortnite-like) | Saturated purple, blue, lime, rarity colours (grey, green, blue, purple, gold) | Chunky rounded bold, bubbly outline, slight tilt | Loot llamas (avoid), supply drops, storm circles, rarity glows, drop pins | Burbank font, official llama, character skins |
| Tactical / military shooter (CoD-like) | Desaturated olive, black, hazard orange or yellow | Stencil, squared mono, tight caps | Crosshairs, HUD brackets, tape, dog tags, grid coordinates | Franchise logos, real military insignia |
| Sci-fi / space | Deep navy, cyan, magenta | Wide geometric, Orbitron-like, thin lines | Scan lines, UI frames, planets, glyph codes | Copying faction emblems |
| Fantasy RPG | Gold, crimson, forest, parchment | Serif display, engraved, ornamental caps | Crests, runes, shields, maps | Copying house sigils |
| Horror | Black, bone, blood red, sickly green | Distressed serif, scratched | Static, VHS, cracks, eyes | Gore-first thumbnails (platform ad rules) |
| Retro / pixel / indie | Limited palettes (e.g. 4–8 colours) | Pixel fonts, chunky bitmap | Sprites, CRT, dithering | Muddy palettes at small size |
| Racing | Red, black, chrome, chequers | Wide italic, speed lines | Tyre marks, tachometers, flags | Car-maker badges |
| Sports games | Team-neutral bold colours | Athletic block, numerals | Jerseys, scoreboards | League and team marks |
| Cozy / life sim | Pastels, warm cream | Rounded friendly sans | Leaves, tools, hearts | Over-busy texture |
| Esports team | 1–2 strong colours plus black or white | Aggressive sharp sans, monogram | Mascot heads, shields, monograms | Overly detailed mascots that die at 40px |

## Creator content patterns that grow
- **Test or myth formats** (e.g. Nought's GTA myth tests) suit verdict devices: stamps, ✓/✗ badges, "REAL or FAKE?" covers.
- **News recap** suits a dateline or ticker and a consistent episode number.
- **Personality-led** creators suit a monogram or face-free mascot plus a signature colour.
