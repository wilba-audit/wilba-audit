# Brand kit checklist

1. **Positioning line.** One sentence on what the brand does differently, plus a tagline.
2. **Logo suite.**
   - Primary logo, secondary (horizontal or stacked), avatar (1080×1080 PNG, safe inside a circle crop), favicon.
   - Clear space and minimum size.
   - Dark and light versions; one-colour version if needed for merch.
3. **Palette as tokens.**
   - Background, raised surface, line, ink, muted ink, brand, on-brand, accent, status colours.
   - Each with dark and light values and a usage note.
   - Contrast-check every text pair with `scripts/contrast.py` (≥4.5:1).
4. **Type.**
   - Display, body and mono/utility faces, with source and licence.
   - A scale (poster, headline, title, lead, body, small, label).
   - Case and tracking rules.
5. **Signature devices.** 2–4 repeatable elements (e.g. verdict stamps, tape labels, input arrows), with do/don't.
6. **Social templates.**
   - Reel/TikTok cover (1080×1920, with the safe zone for the grid crop).
   - Carousel cover and slides (1080×1350).
   - YouTube thumbnail (1280×720).
   - Banner sizes for YouTube (2560×1440, safe 1546×423), Twitch and X.
7. **Voice.** 3–5 do/don't pairs with real example lines.
8. **Imagery rules.** Own gameplay only; licensed music and stock; AI disclosure; no publisher assets.
9. **IP and legal line.** Not-affiliated wording; trademarks to avoid.
10. **Files.** SVG sources, PNG exports, fonts or links, and a short README naming each file's use.
