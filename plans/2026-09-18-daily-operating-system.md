# Plan: The WILBA Daily Operating System — 5-Hour Battle Rhythm

**Created:** 2026-09-18
**Status:** Draft
**Request:** A clear daily structure that ends the overwhelm — one command that generates the outreach pipeline AND the day's social post, so Jess just executes a time-blocked 5-hour day and gets cash in.

---

## Overview

### What This Plan Accomplishes

Gives Jess a single, repeatable **5-hour daily structure** and **one morning command (`/daily`)** that produces everything she needs for the day: a pipeline snapshot, today's written outreach batch (med spa + warm), freshly-researched prospects (generated by Claude), and one ready-to-film social post. She stops deciding and starts executing. It folds the Cash-Sprint outreach engine together with a streamlined social engine (Artlist + Higgsfield, and Griffin's Loveable script app when ready) under one operating rhythm.

### Why This Matters

Jess is lost, overwhelmed, and underperforming — not from lack of strategy, but from lack of *structure*. The business needs one thing above all: **more conversations → booked discovery calls → cash**. This plan removes every decision between her and that outcome by pre-generating the day's work with Claude and time-boxing it into a rhythm she can run "balls to the wall" for 5 hours without having to think about what's next.

## Design Decisions

### Key Decisions Made

1. **One command, one page, one rhythm.** The entire system reduces to: open `outputs/daily-os/README.md`, run `/daily`, execute the blocks. This is the direct antidote to overwhelm.
2. **Claude generates the pipeline; Jess sends.** `/prospects` uses WebSearch to build the med-spa list against the ICP, so Jess never faces an empty list or the blank-page freeze. Maximum leverage on Claude, per her request.
3. **Time-boxed, not count-boxed.** She asked for 5 hours "balls to the wall." The day is structured as blocks of *time* with clear actions, so momentum — not a daily quota she can fail — is the driver. (Targets still tracked: aim ~20–30 touches, 2+ calls booked/week.)
4. **Outreach before content, every day.** Cash before compounding. Content is the last block, never the first.
5. **Offer = money-back guarantee, priced after the call.** Per Jess: "if we build it and you don't like it, we fully refund you," and the level/price is set after the discovery call. This is the strongest possible risk reversal and removes price from the outreach entirely (sell the call, not the build).
6. **Social streamlined to <60 min:** script (Claude / Loveable app) → film on phone → Claude generates b-roll + cover in Artlist/Higgsfield → Claude writes caption → post. One post/day, or batch a week with `/content-week`.
7. **Content angle = build-in-public / AI authority (default).** It builds credibility for *any* outreach and is the fastest to produce; individual posts can be angled at med spa owners when useful. Keeps her from a second niche decision.
8. **Fold the two plans into one.** The cash-sprint outreach assets are built here so a single `/implement` stands up the whole operating system — no half-built states.
9. **Compliance baked in.** Med-spa + AU Spam Act means the message bank and any content run through `marketing-comms-counsel` (inferred consent, unsubscribe, no health testimonials, no prescription-med/brand terms).

### Alternatives Considered

- **Keep `/daily` as-is (60 min).** Rejected — too light for a 5-hour push and still hospitality-angled.
- **Separate outreach and social into different days.** Rejected — she wants both daily; the rhythm interleaves them (cash first, content last).
- **Scrape prospects with an external tool.** Rejected — WebSearch + Claude research is enough, keeps it in-house, and avoids spam-list risk under the AU Spam Act.
- **Lock a price now.** Rejected per Jess — price is level-based and set after the discovery call; the guarantee carries the offer instead.
- **Two commands (`/sprint` + `/day`).** Rejected in favour of one upgraded `/daily` to minimise what she has to remember; `/outreach`, `/content`, `/prospects`, `/pipeline` remain as the pieces `/daily` orchestrates.

### Open Questions (if any)

None blocking — Jess answered the five (med spa; price after call; has a booking link; 5-hour day; guarantee/refund offer). Defaults applied for the minor ones (content = build-in-public/AI authority; Loveable app slots in when ready; Claude generates prospects via WebSearch). Implementation can proceed immediately.

### Step 4: Rebuild `/daily` as the 5-hour master driver

Modify `.claude/commands/daily.md`.

**Actions:**
- New flow, med-spa-angled, run each morning:
  1. **Pipeline snapshot** — read `scoreboard.md` + `wilba-pipeline.md`: the number (calls booked), follow-ups due today.

  4. **One social post** — produce today's phone-film-ready post (script + shot list + Artlist/Higgsfield b-roll + caption).
  5. **Output the day** — print the **time-blocked 5-hour schedule** + the tick-box checklist, and the single most important action.
- Keep narration to one plain line per step. End with the day's number + one kick.

**Files affected:** `.claude/commands/daily.md`
