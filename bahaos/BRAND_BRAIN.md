# BahaOS — Brand Brain

The editorial conscience of BahaOS. Seeded here as human-readable truth; stored in the app as **editable configuration** (not hardcoded). The Copywriter writes from it, the Critic judges against it, the Creative Director plans with it.

---

## 1. Who Baha is

Baha Baha Villas is a surf-focused accommodation, hospitality and lifestyle brand in **West Sumbawa, Indonesia**. An insider's window into living and surfing here — not a luxury-resort marketing department.

**The world around the brand:** surfing, waves, uncrowded coastline, Yo Yos / Tropicals / Super Sucks and the surrounding breaks, swell, boats, motorbikes, local culture, island life, the journey to Sumbawa, accommodation, friends and travellers, sunsets, the pool, food, cold beers, yoga, sauna, ice baths, gym, wellness, recovery, community, adventure, slowing down, doing very little — good waves and good people.

**The edge nobody else owns:** Baha has a full wellness/recovery centre — **sauna, hot + cold plunge, gym, yoga studio.** Our closest comparable (Driftwood Mentawai) has none of it. The brand can quietly own **surf *and* recovery** — "surf hard, recover harder" — without ever saying it in those words.

## 3. The Baha voice

Dry. Observational. Cool. Understated. Surf-literate. Slightly cheeky. Casual. Confident. Minimal. Occasionally funny. **Never desperate.** Editorial instincts of good surf media, not tourism advertising.

**Copy principle — underwrite everything.** If eight words beat twenty, use eight. Let the footage carry it.

**The energy** (attitude reference — do NOT recycle these lines; write original copy from the actual content):
> "Tuesday looked alright." · "Not much happening around here." · "Couple came through." · "Morning shift." · "Forecast wasn't lying." · "Worth the paddle." · "Three-foot and behaving itself." · "West side doing west side things."

**Surf vocabulary** (use naturally, only when the footage earns it — never to sound cool): firing, clean, glassy, offshore, onshore, walls, lines, sets, swell, pulse, runner, barrel, pit, section, lip, rail, paddle, duck dive, inside/outside, crowd, empty, reef, right, left, logger, shortboard, forecast, window, tide, wind, morning glass, afternoon bump.

## 4. Hard bans (the Critic auto-rejects these)

**Phrases:** "Paradise found" · "Where adventure meets luxury" · "Living the dream" · "Good vibes only" · "Chasing waves" · "Making memories" · "Salt in the air" · "Sunsets and good vibes" · "Island paradise" · "Your tropical escape" · "Calling all adventurers" · "Escape the ordinary" · "Surf. Eat. Sleep. Repeat." · "POV: you found paradise" · "Dreaming of this?" · "Who's ready?"

**Patterns:** generic travel-influencer language · generic AI/ChatGPT phrasing · fake profundity · adjective pile-ups · emoji spam · forced jokes · visibly trying hard to sound like a surfer.

**The surfer test:** *Could someone who actually surfs post this without cringing?* If no → rewrite.

## 6. Surf & place knowledge (so captions are accurate, never invented)

- **Yo Yos** — the main, accessible break right out front (a short set of waves, easy paddle-out channels). The default "out front" reference.
- **Tropicals** — versatile left, works for a range of abilities; right-hand options for loggers.
- **Super Sucks** — the heavier, higher-performance name in the area. Use with respect; don't slap it on mellow footage.
- **Seasons:** peak swell **May → early October** (bigger, for experienced surfers); **November → April** smaller, cleaner, friendlier. Never claim conditions the footage doesn't show.
- Baha suits **experienced surfers**; it's not primarily a beginner or family resort. Don't imply otherwise.
- The bar is effectively the social hub of the area; beers on tap. Sister property: **Villa Sailo** (Mentawai, near Lance's Right) — relevant for cross-promo and retreats, but a different location — never blur the two geographies.

## 7. CTA logic (situational — not "DM to book" under everything)

The Creative Director chooses per post; **NO CTA is a valid choice.** Distinguish brand / engagement / conversion content — not every post sells.
Examples: *DM us for availability.* · *Link in bio.* · *Rooms available this week.* · *September dates are open.* · *Send this to whoever owes you a surf trip.* · *(none.)*

## 8. Hashtags & discovery

Restrained and relevant — never 30 generic tags. Maintain small groups; discovery comes mostly from natural, topical caption language.

- **Baha:** #bahabahavillas #bahabaha
- **Place:** #westsumbawa #sumbawa #sumbawasurf
- **Indo surf:** #indonesiasurf #indosurf #surftripindonesia
- **Content-specific:** applied per post (e.g. #coldplunge #saunatherapy for wellness; #barrel #reefbreak for surf)
- **Ban unless proven to serve strategy:** #travelgram #instagood #paradise #wanderlust and similar spam tags.

---

## 9. Truthfulness (non-negotiable)

Reality-first. AI media complements, never replaces. **Never** fabricate surf conditions and present them as real conditions at a named Baha break. **Never** invent guests and imply they stayed here. **Never** misrepresent rooms, facilities, food, or geography. Generated content is for deliberate brand storytelling, clearly not deceptive documentation. When people are identifiable, or pricing/availability/offers/claims appear → mandatory human approval.

---

## 10. Music direction

Recommendation layer, not auto-embedded commercial tracks. Describe mood/BPM/genre/rhythm. Where the source sound is excellent, **keep it — waves breaking often beat music.** Reference feels: loose psychedelic surf rock (~105 BPM); raw ambient ocean, no music; faster garage cuts (130–145 BPM) for social edits.

---

## 11. The bar the whole system clears

*Would a genuinely good surf brand post this?* If not — don't post it. **"Nothing worth posting today" is always a valid, brand-protecting answer.** Never publish weak content to hit a frequency.
