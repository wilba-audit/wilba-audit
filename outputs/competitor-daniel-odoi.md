# Competitor Teardown: Daniel "Mamba" Odoi — and what WILBA steals from it

**Who:** Daniel Odoi (@danielmambaodoi), Brisbane, Australia. Ex-community/cohort operator ("Time To Build," 12 cohorts). Now selling **AIOS to small–mid businesses** via **aios.tech**. YouTube as "Daniel | AI Automation" (@solaitionsai). Books consults at cal.com/bookme-daniel.

**Why he matters to you:** He's an Australian, non-agency-looking guy selling the *exact* thing you're building, and it's working. He's your proof the market's real — and your cheat sheet for messaging.

---

## What he actually says (his messaging, decoded)

His AIOS pitch in one line:
> *"Map your operations → spot the AI opportunities → implement the high-ROI wins. No technical complexity."*

Notice what he does **not** lead with:
- He doesn't sell "an AIOS." He sells **finding the money that's leaking** and plugging it.
- He doesn't sell "automation." He sells **the audit first** — the map — then the wins.
- He doesn't sound technical. "Without technical complexity" is right in the offer.

That's the whole game. He sells the **diagnosis before the build**. The audit IS the offer. The build is the follow-on.

---

## Where you're already aligned (don't change this)

You landed on the same instincts, which is a good sign:
- **Sell outcomes, not "AIOS."** More direct bookings, less OTA commission, less admin. ✅
- **Audit-first.** Your free AI Audit is your version of his "map your operations." ✅
- **Non-technical is a feature.** "I can't code and I built a business that runs on AI" — same disarming move as his "no technical complexity." ✅

So you're not behind. You're on the same road he is.

---

## Where you BEAT him (this is the important part)

He sells to "small–mid businesses" — **everyone**. That's his weakness. Generic AIOS-for-any-SMB is a crowded, faceless pitch. He has to re-explain the value to every new type of business.

**You niche to hospitality/accommodation.** That means:
1. **You already know their leaks** before the call — 2am enquiries lost, OTA commission bleeding, front-desk admin, multi-language guests. You walk in sounding like you run one.
2. **Your proof compounds.** One surf villa case study (Baha Baha) sells the next ten surf villas. His generic case studies don't transfer as cleanly.
3. **You have a second act he doesn't:** the properties become **retreat venues**. He sells a build and moves on. You build the relationship into a partnership. That's a moat.

**The line that captures it:** *"He helps any business find AI wins. I make surf stays and retreats run themselves — because that's the only thing I do."*

Specialist beats generalist in a DM every time.

---

## Your sharpened messaging (steal his structure, keep your niche)

**Your offer, one line:**
> "I map where your property is losing bookings and hours to manual work, then build the AI that plugs the biggest leak. You see the plan before you pay for the build."

**The 3-beat that works in a post, a DM, or a call:**
1. **Map** — "Let's find where the money's leaking." (the free audit)
2. **Spot** — "Here are your 3 biggest AI wins, ranked by ROI." (the audit result)
3. **Build** — "I'll build the top one for you." (the paid work)

Sell beat 1. Never open with beat 3.

---

## Outreach angle (what to actually send)

Lead with the **map**, not the build. The winning DM shape:

> "Hey [name] — I help surf stays / retreats stop losing bookings to slow replies and OTA fees. I put together a free 3-minute AI audit that shows exactly where a property like yours is leaking money + the top 3 fixes. Want me to run yours? No pitch, just the map."

Why it works:
- It's his "map your operations" move, aimed at one niche.
- It gives before it asks (your Albert instinct).
- "No pitch, just the map" kills the sales-guard.
- It's outcome language (losing bookings, OTA fees), never "AIOS."

Go watch his YouTube video **"The AI Offer You Can Sell Tomorrow Morning."** Not to copy the offer — to copy how he *frames selling the audit as the product.* That framing is the unlock. Then run your outreach batch with the "just the map" DM above.
