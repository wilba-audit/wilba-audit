# VerdictStamp

The verdict on a claim, shown as a tilted, distressed rubber stamp: CONFIRMED, UNVERIFIED, BUSTED or EXPIRED. Use one per claim, on post covers, thumbnails and the ledger, and only after the claim has been tested or sourced. The consumer supplies the uploaded stamp SVG (`assets/Stamps`): `-dark` on Night, `-paper` on Paper. Place it over the proof screenshot at about a third overlap, at 30–45% of the frame width.
