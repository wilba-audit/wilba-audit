| Category | **Gaming Video Creator** (show category on profile: on) | Tells visitors what the account is at a glance |

Until the website is live, link to **one email sign-up page** ("Ledger Alerts: get a message the moment a GTA 6 code is confirmed"). Beehiiv or Kit free tiers both give you a landing page in about 10 minutes. Instagram allows up to 5 links. Add in this order:
1. Code alerts (email sign-up)
2. Website / The Ledger (once live)
