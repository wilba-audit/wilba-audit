## 1. Business and audience strategy

### 1.1 Positioning
> **"Every GTA 6 code we list, we've tested on camera. Every code has a date, a source, and a status."**

The site is a fan-run verification desk, not a general GTA news site. Two products from one engine:
1. **The Ledger (website):** a status table of every cheat, promo, bonus and offer claim, each with Confirmed, Unverified, Expired or Unavailable, plus a public verification log and corrections log.
2. **The Tests (YouTube to Shorts, Reels and TikTok):** firsthand test videos that are both the proof for the Ledger and the content.

### 1.2 Audience (assumed, to be validated with real data)
| Segment | Need | Where they are |
|---|---|---|
| Launch players (console, casual) | "Do cheats exist? How do I enter them? Will they break trophies?" | Google, YouTube search, TikTok search |
| Offer hunters | "How do I claim my GTA+ month / pre-order pack? Is this code real?" | Google, Reddit |
| Scam-exposed players (often young) | "Is this free GTA$ generator real?" (No.) | TikTok, YouTube comments |
| Future PC players | PC cheats, trainers vs. built-in cheats | Google, after PC launch [VERIFY when announced] |

[ASSUMPTION] English-speaking, mostly US/UK/AU, console-first at launch. Assume part of the audience is under 18. That affects ads, data collection, tone and sponsorships (see §1.5).

### 1.3 Differentiators
1. **A visible verification method:** platform, game version and date tested, with a clip timestamp.
2. **A public corrections log.** Almost no competitor has one.
3. **"Claims we're testing" kept separate from confirmed codes.** Unverified rumours are never shown as working codes.
4. **Scam-awareness content** (fake "money generators", phishing "promo codes"). This is useful, ethical and shareable.
5. **Speed with proof:** test, clip and update the ledger within hours of a credible claim.

## 2. GTA 6 accuracy and trust system

### 2.1 Status definitions (used on the site, in the ledger and in videos)
| Status | Meaning | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Works as described on the named platform and game version | (a) Firsthand test with a recorded clip or screenshot **plus** platform and game version and date, **or** (b) an official Rockstar or platform-holder source URL. Best is both. |
| **Unverified** | Claimed somewhere, not yet tested or officially published | Source of the claim and date seen. Shown **only** in the "Claims we're testing" section, never in the main table. |
| **Expired** | Was confirmed, but the end date has passed or it no longer works after a patch | Date of expiry or re-test and the evidence |
| **Unavailable** | Doesn't exist, debunked, or not applicable (for example PC before release) | The reason and what we checked |

### 2.2 Verification SOP (claim to published)
1. **Intake:** the Research agent logs a claim to `code-ledger.csv` as *Unverified*, with the source URL and date seen.
2. **Source check:** is there an official source (Rockstar Newswire or Support, PlayStation, Xbox, Rockstar Store)? If yes, record the URL and quote the exact wording.
3. **Firsthand test (human Tester):** run it on a console with the current patch, using a fresh save if possible. Record the full input to the result. Note side effects (trophies or achievements disabled, saving disabled) and record the game version number.
4. **Second check:** a different person or a second test session confirms it. For promo or offer claims, re-read the official terms page.
5. **Status decision:** the Editor-in-Chief (human) sets *Confirmed*. **The AI is not allowed to set Confirmed.**
6. **Publish:** the ledger row is updated with `date_checked`, `checked_by` and `evidence`. The verification log entry is generated automatically from the diff.
7. **Re-check cadence:** after every game patch, all cheats are re-tested (a top-10 smoke test within 24 hours, the rest within a week). Promo and offer rows are re-checked weekly and on their expiry date.

### 2.3 If there are no legitimate codes yet
Never fill the gap with guessed codes. Publish instead:
- **An honest status page:** "As of [date], Rockstar has not confirmed GTA 6 cheats. Here's what we're testing, and how cheats worked in past games." Past-game cheats should be clearly labelled as past-game.
- **Real "codes" that do exist:** the pre-order bonus and GTA+ claim guide (F4), with its deadlines.
- **A scam guide:** "Fake GTA 6 money codes and generators: how to spot them".
- **A sign-up:** "Get an email the moment a cheat is confirmed."

### 2.4 Corrections process
- Every page has a "Report an error" link to a form, which opens a corrections issue.
- Acknowledge within 24 hours, re-test, and update the ledger. Public entry in `/corrections/` shows the date, what was wrong, what changed and why.
- A video error gets a pinned comment, a description correction and the end-screen card updated. If it is material, re-cut the clip and delete or replace the wrong Short.
- Each month, track the **correction rate** (corrections ÷ ledger rows published) as a KPI. A low but nonzero rate is healthy and honest.

### 2.5 Files
- `code-ledger-template.csv`: the single source of truth (schema in §3.6)
- `.claude/skills/gta6-code-verifier/`: the agent SOP for intake and verification

### 3.7 Keyword research workflow (no invented volumes)
1. **Seeds:** "gta 6 cheats", "gta 6 cheat codes ps5", "do gta 6 cheats disable trophies", "gta 6 promo code", "gta 6 pre order bonus", "gta 6 money generator", "gta+ free month".
2. **Expand for free:** Google autocomplete, People Also Ask, related searches, YouTube and TikTok search suggestions, Google Trends (relative interest only), Reddit and forum questions.
3. **Validate with real data:** after launch, Search Console query reports are the source of truth. Optionally Ahrefs or Semrush (paid) later, recording each estimate as *"tool estimate, date"* and never as fact.
4. **Score each query:** intent (informational, offer, scam-check), our ability to add **tested** value, freshness need, and whether it deserves its own page or an anchor.
5. **Map it** in `content-map.md`: query, target page, intent, status (planned, drafted, published), last updated.

### 3.8 Freshness system
- Every page has a visible **"Last checked: DATE"** (the newest `date_checked` in its ledger rows) and a separate "Last updated".
- A daily staleness job (Automation A3) flags confirmed rows not checked in more than 14 days, offers past `expires_on`, and pages whose title date is older than the ledger.
- On every game patch: a smoke test (top cheats), a ledger update and a verification log entry.

### 7.2 Review rhythm
- **48-hour check** on each video: hook and thumbnail decision only.
- **Weekly (30 min):** Analyst scorecard, then pick **one** thing to double down on, **one** to fix and **one** experiment.
- **Monthly:** prune or merge weak pages (thin, cannibalising, out of date), review monetisation readiness and review team and agent performance.
- **Decision rule:** change one variable at a time. Need 3 or more data points before calling a pattern. Treat early numbers as directional, not proof.
