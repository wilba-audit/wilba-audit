## Headline
Not a followers problem — an audience was built, then reach collapsed to near-zero. The gap is **reach + funnel**, not more followers.

## What the data means
1. **June surge, then a cliff.** Big spike in views/reach/followers late May–June, then reach fell from ~2,268 to 20. Follower growth flatlined (+20 in the last month). Whatever drove June wasn't sustained.
2. **Vanity followers.** ~1% of followers now see posts. The spike likely brought low-engagement followers (giveaway/paid-style), which the algorithm then throttled — or posting went quiet. The audience isn't seeing the content.
3. **Virality captured nothing.** ~44k combined June views, no lead magnet/funnel → zero measurable pipeline. Rented attention evaporated.
4. **Split effort.** Two accounts; Dr Gina (2,356) is the bigger, more human asset. People follow people.

## Seven-lens scores
- Positioning & attractive character: 3.5/5 — strong positioning; Gina the bigger asset; effort split.
- Offer & value ladder: 2/5 — discovery call exists; no lead magnet.

## Priority moves
1. Stop chasing followers. Fix reach + build the funnel. Consolidate around Dr Gina as the face; consistent optimised content; a lead magnet so reach becomes a list.
2. Reverse-engineer June and systematise what worked.
3. Park Facebook organically; hold for paid/retargeting to the 45–65 patient.
