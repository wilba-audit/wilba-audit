# Artlist Automation — what it can (and can't) do for you

> Short answer to "can Artlist automate this?": **it automates the production, not the posting.** I use Artlist to make the voiceovers and b-roll so your faceless reels don't need you on camera. You (or a scheduler) still assemble in CapCut and post.

---

## What Artlist automates ✅
- **Voiceovers** (`generate_voiceover`) — turn a script into spoken narration. This is what makes **faceless reels** possible: script → narrated audio, no filming.
- **B-roll video** (`generate_video`) — the visual clips that play under the voiceover.
- **Images** (`generate_image`) — backgrounds and simple covers.

## What it can't ❌
- **Text baked into images** is unreliable — put post text on in **Canva or CapCut**, not the AI image.
- **Auto-posting** — Artlist can't publish to Instagram. Assembly is **CapCut**; scheduling is **Meta Business Suite** (free).

---

## The faceless-reel recipe (repeatable, no filming)
1. **Script** — from `/content-batch` (or `/content`).
2. **Voiceover** — I send the script to Artlist → narrated audio.
3. **B-roll** — I generate 3–5 clips (Artlist or Higgsfield) matching the lines.
4. **Assemble in CapCut** — voiceover + b-roll, auto-captions on, a beat of music.
5. **Export 9:16** → schedule.

I do steps 2–3 for **every faceless post in a `/content-batch` run** — you just assemble.

## Artlist vs Higgsfield — which for what
- **Artlist:** voiceovers + b-roll clips (the faceless-reel workhorse).
- **Higgsfield:** covers/thumbnails, image-to-video, a trained avatar of you later, virality prediction.
- **Your real face** (the 5 talking-heads) is the hero — AI is the polish around it, never a replacement.

## If the Artlist MCP is down (fallback)
I hand you the exact **script + b-roll prompts + voiceover text**, and you run them in the **Artlist app** directly (same result, 5 extra minutes). Nothing blocks the weekend.
