# The Social Engine — one post a day, under an hour

> Content is the LAST block of the day, never the first. Its job right now: build credibility so your outreach converts. Keep it simple and consistent — a phone-shot reel beats a polished one that never gets posted.

---

## The 5-step pipeline (repeat daily)

**1. Script (Claude does this)**
Run `/content` → I pull today's angle (from The Rundown via `trend-scout`, or the next build-in-public idea) and hand you **3 hook options + a 20–30s script + a shot list**.
→ *When Griffin's Loveable app (Rundown → scripts) is live, that becomes your script source — paste its script in and skip to step 2.*

**2. Film (you, on your phone, ~10 min)**
Follow the shot list. Talk to camera for the hook + key lines. Natural light, no ring-light look. One clean take is enough.

**3. Polish (Claude generates the b-roll + cover)**
`visual-producer` makes the supporting shots in **Artlist or Higgsfield** to cut around your talking-head, plus a cover/thumbnail. You don't make these — I do.

**4. Caption (Claude writes it)**
I hand you the caption + hashtags + the one CTA, in your voice.

**5. Post (you)**
Drop your clip + my b-roll into CapCut (auto-captions on), export 9:16, post to **Instagram + LinkedIn**. Done.

**Total: ~45–60 min once you've filmed.**

## Which studio for what
- **Higgsfield** → thumbnails/covers, faceless b-roll, image-to-video, virality prediction, a trained avatar of you later.
- **Artlist** → b-roll clips, image edits, voiceovers.
- **Your real face is the hero** — AI is only the polish *around* your talking-head, never a replacement.
- **Screen recordings** of a system/skill working = your strongest proof shot. Include one whenever the post is about a build.

## The content angle (default)
**Build-in-public / AI authority.** "I can't code and I built a business that runs on AI — here's how." This builds trust for *any* outreach (med spa or otherwise). Occasionally angle a post straight at clinic owners ("the patients who never rebooked…") to warm the niche.

Rotate through: a build-in-progress clip · a "here's a system I made" demo · a behind-the-scenes · one useful AI tip. Post **3–5×/week**.

## Batch a week in one sitting (optional, recommended)
Run `/content-week` → I plan 5–7 posts, you film them all in one session, I make all the visuals. A week of content in ~90 min. Best way to stay consistent without daily filming.

## Go big: the weekend sprint (30 posts)
For a month-long buffer, run the **Weekend Content Sprint**: `outputs/content/weekend-sprint/README.md` → `/content-batch 15` (×2 across the weekend). 30 posts **produced and scheduled out ~1/day** (don't dump them all at once). On camera once. Artlist automates the faceless production — see `outputs/social/artlist-automation.md`.

## The rules
- **Outreach before content, always.** If a day is short, drop content, not outreach.
- **Consistency > perfection.** Posted-and-imperfect beats perfect-and-never.
- Everything checks against `outputs/brand/brand-positioning.md` (no em-dashes, no "unlock/dive in", real voice).
- AU compliance: if a post ever touches the med-spa/health side, no prescription-med brand names, no before/afters, no testimonials.
