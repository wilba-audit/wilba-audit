## 1) LinkedIn connection request (≤300 chars, NO pitch)
**A — warm & specific (send this one):**
> Hi [Name] — [property] looks unreal, that [wave/view/room] 😍. I'm in the surf/wellness world too (based at Baha Baha in Sumbawa right now) working on the AI + marketing side. Would love to connect. 🤙

**B — peer-to-peer:**
> Hi [Name] — fellow hospitality + wellness person here (currently at Baha Baha, Sumbawa). Love what you've built at [property]. Always keen to connect with people doing it well in this space.

## 2) First DM (after they accept — GIVE, don't sell)
**A — the free-audit gift (best):**
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]! Genuinely love what you've built at [property]. No pitch — I run WILBA, we help surf/wellness spots use AI to handle guest enquiries 24/7 and fill more direct bookings. I do a free AI audit of a property's setup — want me to run one for [property]? Zero strings, just genuinely useful. 🙏

**B — softer opener:**
> Thanks for connecting! Quick genuine question — what eats most of your team's time day-to-day: the guest inbox, or keeping the socials alive? Ask because I build AI that quietly takes one of those off your plate, and I love seeing how different places run.

## 3) Cold email (when you have their email)
**Subject lines (pick one):**
- `quick idea for [property]`
- `free AI audit for [property]?`
- `the guest-inbox time-sink`

**Body (3–4 lines, value-first):**
> Hi [Name],
> [Property] looks incredible — saw you're booking up for [season].
> Most surf & wellness spots I talk to lose hours a week to repetitive guest emails, and a chunk of revenue to OTA commissions. I built a free AI audit that shows exactly where a property's leaking time + money (takes me 5 mins, it's genuinely useful either way).
> Want me to run one for [property]?
> — Jess, WILBA · wilba.ai

## 4) The 5-touch follow-up sequence (this is where the money is)
Silence ≠ no. Space these out. Each adds NEW value.

- **Touch 1 — Day 1:** the first DM/email above
- **Touch 2 — Day 3:** *"No worries if now's not the time! Here's a 60-sec example of what the audit turns up [link/loom] — thought it might be useful regardless."*
- **Touch 3 — Day 7:** a proof nudge: *"We just helped Baha Baha [result — e.g. cut guest-reply time / grow social +8K]. Reckon [property] could get a similar win. Want the free audit?"*
- **Touch 4 — Day 12:** switch channel (email→LinkedIn or vice versa): *"Circling back once in case this got buried — still happy to run that free audit whenever."*
- **Touch 5 — Day 18:** the graceful close: *"I'll stop cluttering your inbox 🙂 If it's ever useful down the track, I'm here. Either way — [property] is doing it right."*

## 5) Job outreach (same energy, different target)
When you DM a hiring manager / founder of a remote marketing/AI role:
> Hi [Name] — saw [company] is hiring for [role]. Had to reach out: I'm a marketer who also *builds* the AI systems (10 yrs, ex-UberEATS, now running my own AI automation agency). I'd love to throw my hat in — is it worth a quick chat? Portfolio + CV here: [link].

## 📆 Daily floor (from the 90-day roadmap)
- 10 thoughtful comments on ideal-client posts
- 5–10 connection requests (message A)
- 10–15 first DMs / follow-ups
- **Bad day minimum: 5 touches. Never zero.**

## Remember
- Reply rates of 3–8.5% are *normal* — warmed-up + personalised beats it. It's a numbers game measured in months, not days.
- Rejection is just data. A "no" clears the pipe for a "yes."
- You're not begging — you're offering something genuinely useful (a free audit). Own that.
