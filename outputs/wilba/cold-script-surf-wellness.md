# Cold Outreach Script — Surf & Wellness Resorts
**Country:** Indonesia (English — owners mostly English-speaking)
**Niche:** boutique surf & wellness resorts / surf camps / retreats
**Offer:** free guest-reply + booking widget for the website (free for a testimonial)
**Pricing model:** free for testimonial (upsell handled later)
**Rule in play:** never say "AI" — sell the outcome (instant replies, more direct bookings)

> Your channel is **Instagram DM + WhatsApp**, so the **DM script is your primary**. The call script is here too, for any prospect happy to hop on a quick call.

---

## 📱 DM SCRIPT (your primary — IG / WhatsApp)
*Send as separate short messages, not one block.*

**Opener (message 1):**
> Is this the best place to ask a quick question?

*(wait for reply)*

**Hook (message 2):**
> I was on [resort]'s site and honestly it looks unreal 🌊 Quick one — when a guest messages asking about availability or pricing, how fast do they usually hear back?

**Need question (message 3):**
> Do enquiries ever come in overnight or while you're slammed, and sit for a few hours before someone can reply?

**Need question (message 4):**
> Do you reckon more of them would book direct if they got an instant answer, instead of waiting and drifting to Booking.com?

**Pitch (message 5):**
> I don't know if it'd even make sense for you, but for a little business project I could set you up a free thing on your site that instantly answers guest questions and takes bookings 24/7, in any language.

**Pitch (message 6):**
> Instant answers, more direct bookings, way less inbox. And only if you actually like it, you give me a testimonial. Would that be worth a look?

**Next step:**
> Awesome. I can set it up for [resort] and show you how it'd look. Could you see it tomorrow or the day after? What's usually a good time — and the best email for a quick invite?

**Soft objection handling:**
> No worries. Mind if I ask why — is it just the time, trust, or you already have something in place?

*(pause, hear them out)*

> Totally fair. That's why I usually just make it and show you what it could look like first. I'll build it and show you in 5 to 10 minutes, and if you don't like it, no worries at all.

## ☎️ COLD CALL SCRIPT (for anyone happy to hop on)

### 1. Opener
> Hey, this is Jess. The reason I'm reaching out is I'm working on a business project with surf and wellness resorts around [area]. I was on your site and there's no way for guests to get answers or book instantly — how do people usually get their questions answered and book with you?

### 2. Need-creating questions
1. Ahh I see, so it's mostly email and DMs. Do guests sometimes have to wait a while for a reply back from you?
   *(wait)*
2. Do you ever get enquiries overnight or when you're busy that sit before anyone can reply?
   *(wait)*
3. Do you think more people would book directly if they could get answers and book immediately on your site, instead of going through the OTAs?
   *(wait)*

### 3. Casual pitch layup
> I don't know if this even makes sense for you, but for my project I could set something up for your resort that:
> - instantly answers guest questions — availability, pricing, packages
> - takes enquiries and bookings 24/7, in any language
> - and it's free. You only pay with a testimonial, if you like it.
> Would that be worth seeing?

### 4. Low-pressure next step
> Awesome. I can set it up for your resort and show you how it would look.
> Could you see it tomorrow or the day after?
> What's usually a good time for you?
> What's your email? I'll send you an invite.
> I've sent you a calendar invite — can you check it looks right and accept it if it does?

### 5. Soft objection handling
> No worries. Mind if I ask why — is it just the time, trust, or you already have something in place?

*(pause, acknowledge)*

> Totally fair. That's why I usually just make it and show you what it could look like first. I'll make it and then show you in 5 to 10 minutes. And if you don't like it, no worries.

---

### Notes for Jess
- **Never say "AI," "chatbot," "automation," or "system" in these messages.** Say what it *does* — "instantly answers guest questions." Selling the outcome converts far better cold.
- Add the guarantee anywhere it fits: *"and if you ever worked with me and didn't love it, I'd refund every dollar."*
- The "free for a testimonial" foot-in-the-door is your lowest-friction way to land the first case studies.
