## The outreach engine (headline version — full detail in the outreach skill)
1. **Content first.** Post daily so when you DM, they already half-know you.
2. **Warm before cold.** Like + thoughtfully comment on a prospect's posts *before* connecting.
3. **Value, not pitch.** First message gives a micro-insight or a free resource — never "can I sell you something."
4. **Small ask.** "Worth a quick 15?" beats "let's book a strategy call."
5. **5–7 touches, multi-channel.** LinkedIn + email + a follow-up. Most replies come after touch 3.
6. **Track everything.** A simple list: name, business, last touch, replied?
