# WILBA — Daily Outreach Routine + Social Plan
## The one page you follow every day. Outreach leads, social supports.

**Split:** ~80% outreach (the cash engine) / ~20% social (credibility + long game).
**The rule:** when in doubt, do outreach. Content compounds; outreach pays.

---

## ☀️ The Daily Outreach Routine (30–45 min, same every day)

**1. Open your lists** — `wilba-prospect-list.md` (warm/local) + `wilba-bali-prospects.md` (Bali, with audit angles).

**2. Pick today's targets** — 5 new + any follow-ups due. Warm/local first, then Bali.

**3. 2-minute glance at each** — open their Instagram / booking page. Spot the leak: Only bookable via Booking.com? DMs slow? Socials gone quiet? *That's your opener.*

**4. Send the audit-led message** — lead with *their* leak, offer the free 3-min audit, sell the **quick call** (not the build). DM on Instagram or email.
> *"Had a look at [property] — you're taking bookings mostly through Booking.com, which usually means 15–20% in commission. I build the system that fixes that. Free 3-min audit of your exact leak?"*

**5. Log it** — every send goes in `wilba-pipeline.md` ("outreach sent" + date).

**6. Follow up** — anyone you messaged 2–3 days ago with no reply. **The fortune is in the follow-up** — most replies come on touch 2–4, not touch 1.

**7. Any reply → book the call** — offer the free audit, then a 15-min chat.

**The number: 10 touches a day.** That's the whole job. Say **`/outreach`** and I'll write the day's batch. Log replies with **`/pipeline`**.

---

## 📞 The Discovery Call (when they say yes)
Not a pitch (Ottley's rule). Your job is to **understand**:
- Ask about their bookings, their busiest enquiry times, what's slipping through.
- Talk **outcomes** (more direct bookings, hours back), never tech.
- If they know what they want → quote it. If they don't (most) → **the offer is the audit** → the audit opens the build + retainer.
- End with the price, then **go silent** (Hormozi's 8-second pause).

---

## 📱 How to attack social (the supporting play — keep it light)

**Its job right now:** make your outreach convert (credibility) + slowly build an audience. NOT lead-gen yet.

- **Post 3–4×/week**, all hospitality-angled:
  - The **Baha Baha build in progress** ("building an AI system for a surf villa in Sumbawa…")
  - "**What I built for a surf property**" — show the guest agent / the audit
  - **Behind-the-scenes** — surf-coast founder building AI for beautiful properties
  - One **hospitality AI tip** (how to stop losing after-hours bookings)
- Use your **Content Engine + scripts** (already built) — just angle them to hospitality.
- **Consistency > perfection.** A phone-shot reel beats a polished one that never gets posted.
- **Never let social eat your outreach block.** Outreach first, social second.
