# Monkey Joe's — Facebook & Instagram Marketing Proposal

**Prepared by:** WILBA · **Date:** July 2026 · **Locations:** Pointe Orlando + Winter Park
**Scope:** A focused Facebook/Instagram-only program — top-of-funnel offers + retargeting — replacing broad Google spend.

---

## 1. Why Facebook-only (what the pilot data says)

The pilot ran Google Search + Facebook side by side. The data is clear: **Facebook is the more
efficient channel for actual redemptions.**

| Metric | Google Ads | Facebook / Instagram |
|---|---:|---:|
| Spend (lifetime) | $2,957 gross / $2,057 net | $1,071 |
| Cost per lead | ~$20 (conv.)* | **$3.59** |
| **Redemptions attributed** | 14 | **21** |
| **Cost per redemption** | ~$147 | **~$51** |

\*Google conversion tracking had a known form-tracking bug, so Google's "conversions" are
directional. Facebook drove **more redemptions at roughly a third of the cost.**

**Three more reasons to consolidate on Facebook:**
1. **Parties can't work on Google.** "Birthday parties Orlando" is $25–40 per click on search —
   unaffordable. On Facebook we reach the exact parent audience for ~$3–4 a lead.
2. **Retargeting is barely switched on** — the highest-ROI audience (people who already engaged)
   has had almost no spend. That's the single biggest untapped lever.
3. **Winter Park over-performs on Facebook** (cheaper leads, more redemptions), so budget can be
   weighted to the efficient market.

---

## 2. The strategy — two offers, top-of-funnel + retargeting

**Top of funnel (cold prospecting)** — two proven offers to fill the audience:
- **A. 50%-off admission** — the best-performing offer in the pilot, drives weekday walk-ins.
- **B. Birthday parties ($25 off / `BDAY25`)** — the high-value product, never yet advertised.

**Retargeting (warm)** — convert the audience the top-of-funnel builds:
- Re-engage everyone who watched a video, visited the site, opened the offer, joined the list, or
  redeemed before — with the offer *and* the birthday-party push.

```
COLD  →  50%-off ad  ┐                         ┌→  redeem in-store (walk-in)
        Birthday ad  ┴→  RETARGET warm audience ┤
                                                └→  book a party (BDAY25 / lead form)
```

---

## 3. Campaign structure

| Campaign | Stage | Audience | Offer | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **50%-off — Prospecting** | Cold | Parents 25–45, ~15mi radius each location, kid/family interests | 50% off admission | Cheap leads + walk-ins |
| **Birthday — Prospecting** | Cold | Parents of 2–10s, ~15mi, upcoming-birthday signals | $25 off party / lead form | Party enquiries |
| **Retargeting — All warm** | Warm | Site visitors, video viewers, page/IG engagers, email+SMS list (uploaded), past redeemers | Both offers + party | Redemptions + bookings |
| **Lookalikes** *(phase 2)* | Cold | 1–3% lookalike of redeemers | 50% off | Scale what works |

- **Placements:** Facebook + Instagram feeds, Stories, Reels (auto).
- **Per location** — POL and WP run as separate ad sets so budget and results are clean by store.

---

## 4. Creative angles (from what's worked)

- **"Active kids = happy hearts ❤️"** — Michael's best-ever performer.
- **"Get the kids off the screen"** — indoor active play.
- **Air-conditioned indoor fun** — strong for the hot, rainy Florida summer.
- **Zero-stress parties** — "you bring the cake, we do the rest."
- Format: short **Reels/video** of the play floor + party room, plus static offer cards. Refresh
  creative every 2–3 weeks to beat fatigue.

---

## 5. Budget (recommendation)

Hold total spend near the current level and move it to where it converts:

| | Per location / month | Both locations |
|---|---:|---:|
| 50%-off prospecting | $300 | $600 |
| Birthday prospecting | $200 | $400 |
| Retargeting | $250 | $500 |
| **Total** | **$750** | **$1,500 / month** |

*Weighted slightly to Winter Park once data confirms its edge. Retargeting scales up as the warm
audience grows.*

---

## 6. Tracking & measurement (so every dollar ties to a redemption)

- **Meta Pixel + Conversions API** firing on the offer, redemption, and party pages.
- **UTMs on every ad** → captured in GHL so each redemption attributes to Facebook (this is already
  working — the pilot proved it).
- **Add a redemption date + channel tag** in GHL so redemptions trend weekly by source.
- Weekly scorecard: spend → leads → redemptions → parties, by location.

---

## 7. KPIs & targets (first 90 days)

- **Cost per lead:** hold ≤ $4 (pilot: $3.59)
- **Cost per redemption:** ≤ $50, improving toward $35 as retargeting scales
- **Redemptions:** grow the ~21/period FB-attributed number 3–4× as retargeting turns on
- **Party enquiries:** establish the baseline (currently 0 advertised; 25 warm voice-agent calls
  already waiting)

---

## 8. Why this is the right next step

The pilot proved the model: **$4,027 spend → 479 codes → 101 redemptions.** Facebook did the heavy
lifting per dollar, and the two biggest levers — **retargeting** and **parties** — have barely been
touched. Consolidating on Facebook, with 50%-off and birthdays feeding a retargeting engine, spends
the same money on the channel that actually drives redemptions and bookings.
