5 · Creative direction

What the visuals should be

  • Use your real footage. The play floor, kids on the slides, a party room set up, a genuine birthday moment. Real beats stock every time for a local venue.
  • Reels first. Short vertical video (9 by 16) for Feed, Stories and Reels, plus a couple of simple square offer cards.
  • Hook in two seconds. Open on the action (a kid mid slide), not a logo.
  • Offer on screen. "50% off" or "$25 off parties" overlaid clearly, with the same wording as the ad copy.
  • Refresh every two to three weeks so it never goes stale.

I can mock up concept visuals if useful, but the strongest creative uses Monkey Joe's own photos and video of the real space.

6 · Campaign setup

How it's wired

SettingHow it's built
StructureThree campaigns: 50% off prospecting, Birthday prospecting, Retargeting. Each split into a Pointe Orlando and a Winter Park ad set.
ObjectiveLeads, optimised toward the offer and party form completions.
PlacementsAdvantage+ across Facebook and Instagram feed, Stories and Reels.
Budget$750 per store per month. $300 to 50% off, $200 to birthdays, $250 to retargeting.
TrackingMeta Pixel and Conversions API on the offer, redemption and party pages. UTMs on every ad (source facebook, medium paid_social, campaign named by offer and store) so each redemption ties back in GHL.
Offers50% off admission and the $25 off party code (BDAY25), live and redeemable.
7 · Go live checklist

What it takes to switch on

  • Confirm the Pixel and Conversions API are firing on the offer, redemption and party pages
  • Build the seven audiences above, upload the list and redeemer files
  • Load the ad copy and creative into the three campaigns, split by store
  • Set UTMs so GHL attributes every redemption to Facebook
  • Confirm the 50% off and BDAY25 codes are live
  • One tracking test end to end, then switch on
