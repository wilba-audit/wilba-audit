Built on what won

The copy is pulled from the data, not guesswork

  • The $25 hook drove the most redemptions of any offer. The Welcome voucher pulled 47, more than anything else, so the $25 idea runs through the birthday offer too.
  • Half price was the efficient winner. Best cost per result on Google and the second most redeemed offer. It leads the walk in campaign.
  • "Active kids, happy hearts" is Michael's best performing line ever. We build on it rather than reinvent it.
  • BOGO barely redeemed. Two redemptions across the whole pilot, so we drop it.
  • Facebook retargeting ran at $0.23 per landing page view, well below benchmark. That is the engine under both campaigns.

Creative direction

How these run

  • The cards above are production ready as static offer ads, square for Feed and vertical for Stories and Reels.
  • For video, cut short Reels from Monkey Joe's own footage of the play floor and party room, with the same offer overlaid.
  • Hook in two seconds. Open on the action, a kid mid slide, not a logo.
  • Refresh every two to three weeks so nothing goes stale, and keep the winning ad running.
