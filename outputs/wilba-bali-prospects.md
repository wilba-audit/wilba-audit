## The audit lens — where Bali surf/accom businesses leak money
Almost every one of these is losing money in the same 5 places. Your job is to name the one that fits them:
1. **OTA dependence** — 15–20% of every booking to Booking.com/Agoda; weak direct-booking capture.
2. **Slow enquiry response** — DMs/emails across timezones go cold overnight; international guests lost.
3. **Channel chaos** — enquiries scattered across IG, WhatsApp, email, OTAs; no single system.
4. **Language** — global guests, English-only replies.
5. **No rebooking** — one-time guests never nurtured into repeat direct bookings.
