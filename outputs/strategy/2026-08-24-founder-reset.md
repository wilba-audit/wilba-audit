## 5. THE OFFER

**Buyer:** Owner-operator of a private clinic, 2–15 staff, £/$500+ consult value, no marketing hire. Decision made by one person — usually the founding doctor, sometimes the practice manager.

**Problem:** Hundreds of patients are overdue for follow-up and nobody is chasing them. New enquiries wait days. Reception is buried in WhatsApp and email. Every week of silence is revenue walking to another clinic.

**Outcome sold:** *"We reactivate your dormant patients and answer every enquiry within the hour — without hiring anyone."* Measured in patients rebooked and revenue recovered inside 30 days.

**Mechanism (never led with):** GHL as the operational spine — patient list segmented, recall sequences, WhatsApp/email/SMS in one shared inbox, AI-drafted replies with a human approving every send, staff trained, consent and privacy handled.

**Price hypothesis:**
- **Reactivation Sprint** — US$4,000 fixed (≈ £3,000 / A$6,000). 3 weeks. Deposit 50% up front.

- Clinic pays its own GHL/WhatsApp/Twilio (pass-through, never absorbed).

Sprint #1 and #2 may go at US$3,000 in exchange for a named case study and a testimonial. Never below that, and never free.
