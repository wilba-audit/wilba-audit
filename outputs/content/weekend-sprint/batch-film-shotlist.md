# The ONE Film Session — shot list

> Film all of this back-to-back in ~30–40 min. One outfit, one setting, natural light (coastal/warm, no ring-light look). Talk to camera. One clean take each is enough — don't chase perfect.

**Setup:** phone at eye level, window light in front of you, quiet room. Film each hook + its lines as a separate clip. Say the hook with energy — the first 2 seconds are the whole reel.

---

## Talking-head clips (5)

### Clip 1 · "Who I am" (Post 1)
- **Hook:** "I build AI that runs businesses — from a laptop on the Surf Coast. Here's what that actually looks like."
- Lines: "I run an AI automation agency. From here — not Silicon Valley." · "My clients are busy service businesses quietly bleeding money to missed calls and slow follow-up." · "I build the AI that plugs it — answers at 11pm, books the appointment, follows up while they sleep." · "I'm not a developer, and I don't talk like one. That's the point. Follow for AI that actually makes sense."

### Clip 2 · Flagship: "I can't code" (Post 2)
- **Hook:** "I can't code. I built an AI that rebooks a business's lost customers anyway."
- Lines: "Every business has hundreds of people who came once and never came back." · "Nobody follows up — it means messaging hundreds of people and answering everyone who replies." · "So I built an AI that does the whole thing. I described what I wanted in plain English. That's it." · "If I can build this, imagine what it could do for you."

### Clip 3 · The leak: "2am" (Post 3)
- **Hook:** "Your business is losing money at 2am — and you can't even see it."
- Lines: "Your customers don't only reach out 9 to 5. They message at night, on weekends, from other time zones." · "If no one replies fast, they book your competitor." · "A slow reply is a sale you lost and never even saw." · "I build the AI that answers instantly, any hour. Want to know what it's costing you? Free audit in bio."

### Clip 4 · Honest build-in-public (Post 4)
- **Hook:** "Six months ago I had zero dollars coming in. Here's what I changed."
- Lines: "I stopped trying to build everything and picked one thing that worked." · "I took a system that already books appointments and pointed it at businesses drowning in leads they can't answer." · "No big launch. Just conversations, every day." · "If you're in the messy middle — this is what it actually looks like."

### Clip 5 · Authority (Post 5)
- **Hook:** "Everyone's using ChatGPT to write captions. I built something that makes that look like a typewriter."
- Lines: "Every morning an AI I built reads the day's news, pulls what my audience cares about, and writes me a post — in my voice." · "I didn't write a line of code. I described what I wanted." · "That's the difference between using AI and building AI that works for you." · "Follow — I show you how, no jargon."
