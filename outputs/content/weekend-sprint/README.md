# Weekend Content Sprint — the run-sheet

> The goal is **30 posts produced and scheduled** by Sunday night — NOT 30 posted this weekend. You build a month-long buffer, then drip it ~1/day. Posting 30 at once tanks your reach.

**Why:** outreach is quiet on weekends, so this is the highest-leverage use of the time — a month of credibility running on autopilot while your weekdays stay on outreach. On camera: **once.**

---

## The format mix (so you film once)
- **10 carousels** — designed in Canva, no filming.
- **10 faceless reels** — Artlist voiceover + b-roll (I produce), no filming.
- **5 screen-record reels** — capture the system working, no face. *Your strongest proof.*
- **5 talking-head reels** — filmed back-to-back in ONE session.

Full list + tracker: `30-post-plan.md`. Film session: `batch-film-shotlist.md`.

---

## 📅 The 2-day schedule

### Saturday AM (~90 min)
- Run **`/content-batch 15`** → I write posts 1–15 in full + generate the Artlist voiceovers/b-roll for the faceless ones.
- While assets render: **design the carousels** in Canva (navy/sand + ocean-blue accent, one thought per slide).

### Saturday PM (~60 min)
- **The ONE film session:** record the 5 talking-head clips from `batch-film-shotlist.md` — back-to-back, one outfit, one setting.
- **Record the 5 screen-captures** (the proof shots — `/daily` running, a booking landing, a skill firing).

### Sunday AM (~90 min)
- Run **`/content-batch 15`** for posts 16–30 + their assets.
- **Assemble the reels in CapCut:** drop in your clip / the Artlist voiceover + b-roll, auto-captions on, a beat of music, export 9:16.

### Sunday PM (~45 min)
- **Schedule all 30** (see below), ~1/day. Mark each **Scheduled** in the tracker.
- Done. Your grid runs itself for a month.

---

## 🗓 How to schedule (free)
- **Meta Business Suite** (business.facebook.com) → Planner → schedule Instagram + Facebook posts for free. Reels + carousels both supported.
- Alt: **Later** or **Metricool** free tier.
- **Cadence:** ~1/day. Front-load the **screen-record + talking-head** proof posts (highest impact). Spread carousels and faceless reels between them.
- **Drip, don't dump.** 1/day for a month beats 30 in a weekend every time.

---

## The rules
- **Consistency > perfection.** A phone-shot, Artlist-polished reel that ships beats a perfect one that never does.
- **Every post = one CTA** (DM a keyword, or "free audit / quick chat" → your Calendly).
- **Voice:** Brand Bible — plain English, real numbers, no em-dashes, no "unlock/dive in."
- **Compliance:** health-adjacent posts → no prescription-med brand names, no before/afters, no testimonials.
- **Weekday tie-in:** once the buffer exists, `/daily` Block 4 just grabs the next scheduled post — protecting your outreach time.
