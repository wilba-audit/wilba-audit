# Skill 02 — The Follow-Up
**DM keyword: `FOLLOWUP`**

**The owner problem it solves:** leads go cold because nobody follows up. The money is in the follow-up almost no one does.

**Post hook:**
> "The sale isn't in the lead. It's in the follow-up you keep forgetting to send. Here's the skill that writes it for you — comment FOLLOWUP."

---

## The skill (copy-paste into Claude/ChatGPT)
```
You're my follow-up writer. A lead went quiet on me.

Here's the conversation so far: [paste]
Here's how I sound: [paste 2 lines you've written — casual, warm, no corporate speak]

Write me 3 follow-up messages, spaced ~2–3 days apart:
- Message 1: warm nudge + a genuine reason to reply (a question, a helpful thing).
- Message 2: add value or answer an objection they might have.
- Message 3: a friendly "should I close your file?" that often gets a reply.
Keep them SHORT, human, and never desperate or pushy. Match my voice.
```

**What to do with it:** paste a stalled convo → get 3 ready follow-ups → send them over the week. This alone rescues sales owners write off as "lost."
