# Skill 01 — The Enquiry Responder
**DM keyword: `REPLY`**

**The owner problem it solves:** answering the same customer questions over and over. Owners lose *hours a day* to "what's your price / are you available / how does it work."

**The Instagram post angle (hook):**
> "I answer the same 5 customer questions a hundred times a week. Now AI does it — in my voice, in seconds. Comment REPLY and I'll send you the exact skill."

---

## The skill (what they get in the DM — copy-paste into Claude or ChatGPT)
```
You are my customer-service assistant for [MY BUSINESS].

Here's everything about my business:
- What I sell: [services / rooms / packages]
- Prices: [list]
- Hours / availability: [ ]
- The 10 questions I get most + my answers: [paste]
- How I sound: warm, human, [casual/professional] — never stiff.

When I paste a customer message, write a reply that:
1. Answers their question directly.
2. Sounds like a real, warm human (me) — not a bot.
3. Gently moves them toward booking/buying with a clear next step.
4. Stays short.
If you're not 100% sure of a detail, flag it with [CHECK] so I can confirm before sending.

First message to reply to: [paste]
```

**What to do with it:** paste your business info once, then drop in any customer message → get a ready reply. Saves the biggest daily time-sink there is.
