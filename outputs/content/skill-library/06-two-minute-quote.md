# Skill 06 — The 2-Minute Quote
**DM keyword: `QUOTE`**

**The owner problem it solves:** writing quotes and proposals eats an hour every time — so they go out slow (or never), and the lead cools off.

**Post hook:**
> "I used to spend an hour on every quote. Now it's 2 minutes and it goes out while they're still keen. Comment QUOTE."

---

## The skill (copy-paste into Claude/ChatGPT)
```
You're my proposal writer. Here are my rough notes from a client chat: [paste]

Write a clean, friendly quote/proposal with:
1. A one-line summary of what they want (so they feel understood).
2. What they get — clear, in plain English (not jargon).
3. The price + what's included.
4. One clear next step.
Professional but warm, like it's from a real person who gets them. Flag anything I need to confirm with [CHECK].
```

**What to do with it:** dump your messy call notes → get a clean quote to send in 2 minutes. Speed wins deals; this makes you fast.
