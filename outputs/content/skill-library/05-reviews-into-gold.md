# Skill 05 — Reviews Into Gold
**DM keyword: `REVIEW`**

**The owner problem it solves:** great reviews and testimonials just sit there, unused. Their best marketing is already written — by their customers.

**Post hook:**
> "Your best marketing is already sitting in your reviews. Here's how to turn one into a week of content. Comment REVIEW."

---

## The skill (copy-paste into Claude/ChatGPT)
```
You're my marketing assistant. Here's a real customer review: [paste]

Turn it into:
1. A warm public reply to the reviewer (grateful, human, not corporate).
2. A social post that celebrates the win without bragging — lead with the customer's transformation, not "we're the best".
3. A short testimonial-graphic caption (the punchiest line from the review + a soft CTA).
Keep my voice: [casual/warm]. Don't invent anything the review doesn't say.
```

**What to do with it:** paste a review → get a reply + a post + a testimonial caption. Turns social proof you already have into marketing that sells.
