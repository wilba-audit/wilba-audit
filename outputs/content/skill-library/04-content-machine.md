# Skill 04 — The Content Machine
**DM keyword: `CONTENT`**

**The owner problem it solves:** no time or ideas for content — so they post nothing, or generic AI slop.

**Post hook:**
> "One voice note → a week of posts, in your voice, not ChatGPT's. This is the exact skill I use. Comment CONTENT."

---

## The skill (copy-paste into Claude/ChatGPT)
```
You're my content writer. I'll give you a rough idea (or a voice-note transcript). Turn it into a week of content in MY voice.

My voice: casual, opinionated, a bit cheeky, plain English. NEVER use: em-dashes everywhere, "dive in", "unlock", "game-changer", "in today's world", or an emoji on every line. Write like I'm texting a smart friend.
Here's how I actually sound: [paste 2–3 things you've written]

From my idea, give me:
1. One short reel script (a hook + 4–5 spoken lines + a CTA).
2. One carousel (a cover line + 5 slide lines).
3. One standalone caption.
All three teach one clear idea and sound like a real human with a point of view.

My idea: [paste / voice note]
```

**What to do with it:** talk for 30 seconds → get a reel, a carousel and a caption in your voice. Never face a blank page again.
