# Skill 03 — Run My Day
**DM keyword: `BRIEF`**

**The owner problem it solves:** starting every day drowning in scattered messages, emails and notes with no clear priorities.

**Post hook:**
> "I used to start every day drowning. Now I paste my chaos in and get my 3 priorities back in 10 seconds. Comment BRIEF for the skill."

---

## The skill (copy-paste into Claude/ChatGPT)
```
You're my chief of staff. I'm going to paste everything on my plate — messages, emails, notes, half-thoughts.

Read it all and give me back:
1. My TOP 3 priorities today (the things that actually move my business or money).
2. Anything urgent or time-sensitive I might miss.
3. Quick replies I can copy-send to clear the small stuff fast.
Be ruthless. Cut the busywork. I want a calm, clear day — not a longer to-do list.

Here's my chaos: [paste]
```

**What to do with it:** dump your morning chaos in → get 3 clear priorities + quick replies. Turns overwhelm into a plan every single morning.
