## Phase 3 — Diagnosis (root causes, not symptoms)

1. **Fragmented presence** `[REAL]` → splits audience + algorithm signal across
   two profiles + a dead page. *Failure pattern: Invisible/Inconsistent identity.*
2. **Feast-or-famine cadence** `[REAL]` → the algorithm never learns the account;
   baseline collapses between bursts. *Pattern: Feast-or-Famine.*
3. **No amplification / distribution** `[REAL]` → content is a broadcast, not a
   conversation; nothing shared, no email capture, so nothing compounds.
   *Patterns: Post-Once-and-Pray + Dead-End.*
4. **Production-value fallacy** `[PROXY]` → spend went to gloss over hooks/retention.
5. **Peer-not-patient voice** `[CONFIRM]` → likely talking to colleagues, not Sarah.

## Phase 4 — Prognosis (honest, no vanity promises)
- **Months 1–3:** consolidate to one profile; fixed 3×/wk cadence; hook system;
  share-engineered formats; email capture. *Leading signs:* reposts 0→several/post,
  steadier baseline views, better DMs.
- **Months 3–6:** consistent qualified reach; first traceable consult enquiries.
- **Months 6–12:** authority compounds; branded search rises; social becomes a
  reliable low-cost channel for premium consults.
- **Won't promise:** a follower number or virality. A system + leading indicators only.
