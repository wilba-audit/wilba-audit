### Instagram findings
1. **The breakout is real and it's the whole story.** The clinic account proves
   the ceiling: 23k views/week and +1,200 followers in weeks. She's **already
   done it once** — this is a slump, not a limit.
2. **★ Find the June winner.** The single most valuable action: identify *what*
   posted in late-May/early-June that drove this (a Reel? a topic? a collab?).
   It's a proven format to rebuild around. **We need the IG post content to see it.**
3. **Two Instagram accounts = split effort.** The clinic account has the
   momentum; the personal account has the size. Running both dilutes both —
   decide a primary and a clear role for each (or consolidate).
4. **Momentum wasn't sustained.** Both accounts fell ~90–97% after June — the
   classic "went viral, didn't have a system to follow through" pattern.
5. **Reach has gone cold** (12–46/week now) — the accounts need re-activating
   with consistent, hook-led content.
