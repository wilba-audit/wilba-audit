## 10. PAID GROWTH ENGINE (Phase 5) — the ads→survey→WhatsApp motion ⭐
**The headline of the pitch — and it reuses infrastructure Gina is already paying for.**
Not "boost posts." A staged, measured acquisition system:
1. **Learn (organic):** identify the strongest problems/hooks/topics from real
   engagement (and the June winner).
2. **Amplify (Meta ads):** put spend behind *proven* messages to reach more of the
   right women/men — health-compliant creative.
3. **Capture (survey/assessment):** ad → a **symptom/needs survey or assessment**
   (compliant, high-intent, educates + qualifies) → lead into GHL.
4. **Follow up (WhatsApp AI agent):** the **already-built** WhatsApp agent + KB
   nurtures the lead, answers operational questions, and routes to a **discovery
   call** — with the **clinical escalation** guardrails already designed. *This is
   the IHF playbook, on infrastructure that already exists here.*
5. **Retarget:** warm audiences from video viewers, engagers, site visitors,
   assessment-starters.
6. **High-intent (Google/Search):** assess whether search ads fit (existing agency
   owns core Google — coordinate).
7. **Measure:** ad/content → visit → enquiry → **qualified** enquiry → consult →
   patient, wherever technically/legally possible. **North-star = cost per qualified
   patient opportunity**, not cheap clicks.

**Why WILBA is uniquely positioned:** the survey→WhatsApp-agent→escalation→recall
spine is *this build*. WILBA can connect acquisition to delivery to data in one rail
— an outside ads agency can't.
