## PAGE 4 — WHAT THE DATA TELLS US (4–6 findings)
- **Purpose:** Turn analysis into a few meaningful insights (no analytics dump).
- **Core message:** Real potential, a few clear gaps, all fixable.
- **Headline:** *"Five things worth your attention."*
- **Copy (the findings, tight):**
  1. **The audience already responded.** The clinic account's June growth proves the appetite is real. *(The job: understand what caused it and make it repeatable — see next page.)*
  2. **Your authority is split across two accounts** (Dr Gina ~2,350 · Clinic ~1,515). Not a problem — an opportunity to give each a clear job instead of running two disconnected calendars.
  3. **LinkedIn has the expertise; distribution can lift it.** Strong, credible, consistent content — with clear room in hooks, format, link placement and repurposing. *(Content review, not a numbers claim.)*
  4. **Facebook isn't where organic effort belongs right now** (very small audiences) — but that says nothing about Meta as a *paid* acquisition platform. Different tool, different job.
  5. **Right now, attention has nowhere intelligent to go** (the big one — page 6).
  6. **The infrastructure to receive that attention is already being built** (the bridge — page 7).
- **Visual:** Five compact finding cards, each one line + one implication.
- **After this page:** *"None of this is fluff — these are real, and they're fixable."*

## PAGE 8 — WHAT THIS LOOKS LIKE FOR A PROSPECTIVE PATIENT
- **Purpose:** Make it human and tangible (and make the assessment feel real without locking a name).
- **Core message:** Content becomes the start of a real, measurable journey.
- **Headline:** *"Imagine one of your future patients."*
- **Copy:** *"A woman sees a short video from Gina naming a problem she's quietly lived with for months. ## PAGE 10 — PAID GROWTH (prove before scale)
- **Purpose:** Make ads exciting *and* safe.
- **Core message:** We learn first, spend big never before the evidence.
- **Headline:** *"Advertising, without gambling."*
- **Copy:** *"Paid growth here doesn't mean 'spend money boosting posts.' It means: **Test** small, controlled campaigns → **Learn** which patient problems and messages attract the *right* people → **Prove** we can create qualified enquiries at a sustainable cost → **Optimise** creative, audience, assessment and follow-up → **Scale** only once the evidence justifies it. We call it **Prove before scale**, and it runs through everything we do."*
- **Visual:** The 5-step ladder (Test→Learn→Prove→Optimise→Scale) with **PROVE BEFORE SCALE** as a banner.
- **After this page:** *"This isn't a gamble. They're testing with small money before asking for big money."*

## PAGE 11 — THE COMPOUNDING ADVANTAGE
- **Purpose:** Show why this beats "post every Monday."
- **Core message:** The system gets smarter; the learning is the asset.
- **Headline:** *"Most marketing starts again every Monday. This gets smarter every month."*
- **Copy:** *"Month 1: which questions attract attention? Month 2: which topics get assessments completed? Month 3: which audiences create qualified conversations? Month 4: which messages lead to consultations? Month 6+: which themes appear to bring in valuable patients? Each answer feeds the next round of content, ads, follow-up and future campaigns. **The asset isn't only the audience we build — it's what the clinic learns about that audience.**"*
- **Visual:** A rising staircase (Month 1→6+), each step a question that feeds the next.
- **After this page:** *"This gets more valuable the longer it runs. That's different from every agency I've seen."*

## PAGE 12 — THE FIRST 90 DAYS (proof phase)
- **Purpose:** Make it concrete and low-risk — evidence, not a forecast to believe.
- **Core message:** In 90 days you'll have real numbers, not promises.
- **Headline:** *"The first 90 days: built to produce evidence."*

We're building a system designed to show you the truth."*

- **After this page:** *"I can picture exactly what the first three months look like — and I'm not committing to a leap of faith."*

- **Purpose:** Frame the commercial shape and the economics we'll establish, without unproven ROI.
- **Core message:** One clean partnership, priced against real economics we build together.
- **Headline:** *"How the investment works — and the economics we'll establish together."*
- **Copy:**
  - **The shape:** *"One growth build to set everything up, and one simple monthly growth partnership — a single number that also absorbs your existing support retainer, so there's one relationship, not a confusing stack.
