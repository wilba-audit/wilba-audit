## The three problems — all in the content, all fixable

### ① Links in the post body are throttling her reach ★ instant quick win
**Every single post** ends with a `lnkd.in/…` or external link *in the body*.
LinkedIn demotes posts that send people off-platform — it's the most common
self-inflicted reach cap there is. **Fix:** move the link to the **first
comment**. This alone often lifts reach materially, overnight, on content she's
*already posting.* Zero new work.

### ② Soft opening lines are wasting strong content
Only the first ~2 lines show before "…see more," so the opening *is* the post.
Hers mostly start with throat-clearing:
- ✗ "Modern healthcare should never be about waiting until something goes wrong."
- ✗ "Biological age is generating a lot of conversation at the moment…"
- ✗ "Last week a randomised, placebo-controlled trial…"

And where she hooks, it works — **"'I've always suffered from IBS.'"** (a patient
quote) and **"These are the blood markers I recommend women over 40 should
check"** (concrete, useful) are her strongest openers, and her document post also
pulled the most *comments*. She has the instinct; it's just not applied every time.

**Hook rewrites (before → after):**
- *Biological age:* → **"Your birth certificate says 52. Your cells might say 61 — or 44. Here's how we measure the gap, and close it."**
- *Prevention:* → **"By the time a blood test flags a problem, it's usually been building for years. Here's what we look for before that."**
- *GLP-1 study:* → **"The weight-loss jab everyone's talking about may also slow ageing itself. As a longevity doctor, here's what I'd actually take from the new trial."**

### ③ Format & voice: her best formats are under-used, her weakest are over-used
- **Article-link posts are her worst performers** (2 and 1 reactions). Native
  formats win: her **document** (8 + 4 comments) and **videos** (6, 14) far
  outperform link-drops. *Do more documents/native video; stop leading with external articles.*
- **Voice drifts to the clinic "we"** rather than **Gina "I."** Her most personal,
  first-person post (the GLP-1 take — "something I find genuinely exciting… the
  people I work with") is her best-written — it's just buried under length and an
  external link. **Lead as the person, not the practice.**
- **Length:** several posts are dense blocks. LinkedIn rewards one idea, short
  lines, white space.
- **Amplification:** her posts aren't being reshared by others (reposts ≈ 0),
  which is why great content stays inside her network. Hooks + native formats +
  a question that invites comments will start to fix this.
