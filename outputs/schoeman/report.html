04 — The plan

Fix the steering and the fuel line.

  • Consolidate to one LinkedIn profile + reactivate the company page to reshare. Ends the fragmentation immediately.
  • Fixed cadence — 3 posts/week on set days. Ends the 30× volatility.
  • Engineer for shares: document carousels, strong clinical POV, and consented patient-outcome stories — the formats that actually get reposted.
  • Hook first, translate for the patient (not the peer) — speak to the 49-year-old told her bloods are “normal.”
  • Own the audience: a lead magnet (“Is it your hormones?”) + email, so reach converts to consults.
  • Leverage the videos you’ve already paid for: re-cut into hook-first clips + carousels — one shoot → ~12 assets, no new spend.
