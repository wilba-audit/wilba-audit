## 11. ECONOMIC MODEL (verified numbers + variables)
**Verified `[FACT]`:** initial consult £350 / £500 / £750; follow-up £250 (×2 types); ~815 patients; ~385 overdue follow-ups. **Unknown (→ variables):** patient contribution value, LTV, conversion rates, current volume, margins, capacity.

**Variables to populate WITH Gina:**
- `V` = year-1 contribution value of an acquired patient (consults + follow-ups + bloods + treatment margin)
- `LTV` = multi-year value (hormone/longevity patients recur — likely ≫ V)
- `Q` = qualified-lead → consultation rate
- `C` = consultation → ongoing-patient rate
- `A→Q` = assessment-completion → qualified-lead rate
- `CPL` / `CPQL` = cost per (qualified) lead · `CPA` = cost per acquired patient
- `S` = monthly ad spend · `R` = WILBA monthly retainer

**How we determine the levers:**
- **Break-even CPA = V** (year-1 contribution). Spend below this and year-1 alone profits; LTV widens the margin.
- **Max sustainable CPA** = target fraction of V (e.g. ≤ 30–40% of V for a healthy premium-clinic margin).
- **Break-even incremental patients (to cover WILBA)** = `R ÷ contribution-per-patient`.
- **Required qualified leads** = `target patients ÷ C`; **required leads** = `qualified ÷ (A→Q)`; **required spend** = `leads × CPL`.

**Illustrative scenario (PLACEHOLDER — Gina confirms every number):**
> *If V = £1,000, C = 60%, Q = 30%, CPL = £40:* one patient needs ~1/(0.3×0.6) ≈ **5.6 qualified-to-consult attempts**… i.e. to net **4 new patients/month** we'd need ~22 consults-worth of qualified leads → model the lead volume and spend from real CPL once tested. **Break-even CPA = £1,000; target CPA ≤ ~£350.** At R = £2,500/mo, break-even is **~2–3 incremental patients/month** — a low bar for a £350–£750 consult. *(Numbers illustrative only; the 90-day test replaces them with real ones.)*
