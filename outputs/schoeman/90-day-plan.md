## Week-by-week

### Weeks 1–4 · Foundations + immediate known wins
- **Wk 1:** Confirm capacity (Gina). Pull the **June breakout** data. Set account roles. **Apply LinkedIn fixes immediately** (links → first comment, hook rewrites, drop article-link posts). Stand up tracking (UTM + tags). Start re-cutting existing video into hook-first clips.
- **Wk 2:** Publish first hook-first clips + document carousels on pillars 1–2. Draft the assessment (compliance review). Bio/positioning tidy on both accounts.
- **Wk 3:** Build the assessment + landing + lead capture. Continue cadence. First consented-lead → WhatsApp handoff mapped with the existing follow-up.
- **Wk 4:** Assessment live end-to-end (tested). Baseline metrics captured. Retargeting audiences started (video viewers, engagers).

### Weeks 5–8 · Launch the engine + first controlled paid tests
- **Wk 5:** Content engine at full cadence across pillars; LinkedIn share-engineered formats (documents, POV, consented stories).
- **Wk 6:** **Launch paid tests** (the 4 angles above), small budget (from $1,000/mo). Assessment as the destination.
- **Wk 7:** Monitor qualified-lead quality; connect leads into the WhatsApp follow-up; first optimisation pass on creative + hooks.
- **Wk 8:** Kill weak angles, double the budget share of the strongest; retarget assessment-starters who didn't finish.

### Weeks 9–12 · Optimise + establish economics + decide
- **Wk 9–10:** Optimise creative/audience/assessment/nurture on real numbers. Track lead → discovery → consult.
- **Wk 11:** Establish baseline **CPL, cost per qualified lead, lead→consult** (patient→ where measurable).
- **Wk 12:** **Decision review:** scale what works, change what doesn't, stop what doesn't justify the spend. Set the next-quarter plan and the sustainable acquisition cost.
