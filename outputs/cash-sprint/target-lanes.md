# Target Lanes — who you reach, in what order

> Warm first (fastest yeses), then ONE cold niche (med spa). Never run more than these. This is the anti-scatter rule.

---

## Lane A — Warm Network (ALWAYS ON, do first)
People who already know you = the fastest path to a call. No cold-start, no spam risk.

**Who:**
- Past clients/colleagues, IHF contacts, Danielle's world, anyone from previous businesses.
- People in your phone, IG followers/following, LinkedIn connections, old email threads.
- Anyone adjacent to health/beauty/clinics — even a "do you know anyone who runs a clinic?" referral ask.

**Criterion:** they know your name. That's it.

**The ask:** a warm, direct message → a quick chat OR "who do you know?" referral. (See `message-bank.md` → Lane A.)

**Daily:** 8–12 warm touches. Refill from your contacts each morning.

---

## Lane B — Med Spa / Cosmetic Clinics AU (PRIMARY cold niche)

**The ICP — YES:**
- Cash-pay (no insurance/Medicare billing) — the qualifying question sorts this.
- Injectables / aesthetics led (treatments wear off on a cycle → predictable rebooking).
- Owner-operated, one location or a small handful.
- Established — a real patient history (roughly 300+ lapsed patients as a floor).
- Already busy and well-reviewed (reactivation amplifies something that works).

**The ICP — NO:**
- Dermatology / plastic surgery (bill insurance, heavier privacy regime).
- Anything inside a hospital or multi-specialty group (buyer's not in the room).
- National chains / franchises (marketing decided at head office).
- Brand-new clinics (no dormant list to work).

**Where to find them (AU):**
- **Google Maps by metro:** Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Gold Coast, Perth carry the density. Filter for independents with a real review history; exclude derm/plastic/chains.
- **Instagram:** the injector is usually the brand → the owner's name is easy to find → DM a person, not an `info@`.
- **Practice-management directories:** Pabau, Fresha, Zenoti public listings. Knowing their platform is useful before the call anyway.

**The leak/opener:** most clinics never work their dormant list and are slow to answer new enquiries. That's the opener — framed as *their* opportunity, not our product. (See `message-bank.md` → Lane B; sell the map, not the build.)

**Daily:** 8–12 cold touches, pulled from `prospects-medspa.md`. Run `/prospects` to refill.
