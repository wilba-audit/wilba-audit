# Discovery Call Script — 15 minutes, book the next step

> Not a pitch. Your job is to understand their world, reflect the leak back in dollars, make the offer, then go quiet. Sell the call's outcome, never the tech.

---

## Before the call (2 min)
- Glance at their IG/site. Note: how busy, how they take bookings, any obvious gap.
- Have `offer.md` open. Booking/next-step link ready.

## The open (30 sec)
> "Thanks for jumping on [name]. No pitch here — I just want to understand how [clinic] handles patients and enquiries, and if there's an easy win I'll show you. Sound good?"

## The qualifying question (ask early)
> "Quick one first — do you bill any insurance or Medicare electronically, or are you cash-pay?"
(No = ideal. Yes = note it; price/park accordingly.)

## Discovery — 4–5 questions (let them talk)
1. "Roughly how many patients have come in once or twice and not been back in 6+ months?"
2. "What's a returning patient worth to you over a year, ballpark?"
3. "When a new enquiry comes in — DM, form, missed call — who answers it, and how fast?"
4. "And who follows up with the ones who go quiet after the first message?" *(answer is almost always "nobody" — that's the sale)*
5. "What have you tried before to bring lapsed patients back?"

## Reflect the leak back — in dollars (30 sec)
> "So you've got roughly [X] lapsed patients, each worth about [$Y]. Even if a small slice come back, that's [$Z] just sitting there — and right now nobody's working it. That's exactly what I set up."

## The offer + guarantee (keep it short)
> "Here's how I work it: I build the system that quietly rebooks that list and answers new enquiries the moment they land. My first client I do free to prove it out, and after that there's a full money-back guarantee — you watch it run on your own patients, and if you don't love it, you get every dollar back. The exact setup depends on your list, so I'll price it once I've seen it."

## Then STOP (8-second pause)
Say the offer, then **go silent.** Let them speak first. Do not fill the gap. This is where the yes happens.

## Book the next step
- If yes → "Perfect. Next step is I map your list and set the build in motion — let's lock [day/time]." Put it in the calendar on the call.
- If maybe → "Totally fine. Want me to send a 3-line recap and check in [date]?" (Log it.)

## After the call (2 min)
- Log outcome in `scoreboard.md` (call held → outcome) + move them in `wilba-pipeline.md`.
- Send a 3-line recap: what you'll do, the guarantee, the next step/date.

---
**The two rules:** (1) sell the outcome, never the tech. (2) Price then pause — always.
