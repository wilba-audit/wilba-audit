# Google-Ads Advertisers Pipeline (high-intent)

> Businesses running Google Ads in the space — they pay per lead, so slow follow-up = wasted spend. The sharpest cold segment. Run `/ad-scan [niche] [city]` to populate/refill.
>
> **Angle:** honest + urgent — "you're paying for these leads, here's how to stop losing them." Email template: `message-bank.md` → **Lane D**. **Never** imply a fault with their account.

**Contact route:** owner IG DM, or the enquiry email on their site (which is often the one their ads point at).
