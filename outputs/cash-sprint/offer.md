# The WILBA Offer — Med Spa (one offer, one guarantee)

> This is the ONLY offer you sell during the sprint. Don't improvise new ones on calls. The guarantee carries it; the price is set after the call.

---

## The one-liner
**"I set up an AI system that quietly rebooks the patients who stopped coming in — and answers new enquiries the moment they land, so none slip through. You only decide anything after you've seen it working. If we build it and you don't love it, you get every dollar back."**

## What it actually does
- **Reactivation:** works your existing patient list — the people who came once or twice and never rebooked — with a real, multi-week text conversation that books the ones who are ready.
- **Fresh enquiry response:** answers every new enquiry in minutes (text + light voice), books it, and follows up forever so nothing goes cold.
- Both handle consent, opt-outs and do-not-contact rules as a core function, not an afterthought.

## The guarantee (the whole reason they say yes)
**Full money-back guarantee.** We build it, you watch it run on your own list. If you don't love it, we refund you in full. Zero risk to them — that's the entire pitch. Lead with it.

## Pricing — decided AFTER the discovery call
Do **not** quote a number in outreach. Price is level-based and set on the call once you understand their list size, systems, and what they need. Rough internal shape (never sent cold):
- A modest setup, then either a share of recovered revenue for the first 90 days → a flat monthly, **or** a flat monthly subscription + setup.
- One number, decided on the call, same logic for everyone. The guarantee — not the price — is what you sell.

## The one qualifying question (ask early)
**"Do you bill any insurance or Medicare electronically?"**
- **No** → ideal (cash-pay keeps it out of the heavier health-privacy regime). Proceed.
- **Yes** → more compliance/cost; either price accordingly or park for later.

## Honesty in the pitch (never overclaim)
- The engine is **proven** — the underlying system has run in production for 5+ months and booked 200+ appointments for a paying client in another industry.
- The **med-spa version is a build** (Griffin builds it; ~6–8 weeks to finished; carrier registration under the clinic's business needs their paperwork and a few days).
- **First client is pro-bono / guaranteed** — we want the case study more than the margin. Say that plainly; it's disarming and true.

## Compliance guardrails (non-negotiable in AU)
- **No prescription-medicine brand names or category terms**, no "before/after" claims, no prices/discounts/packages in any patient-facing message. Reactivation copy is a genuine recall: *"it's been a while since your last visit — would you like to come in?"*
- **No testimonials** in any advertising of a regulated health service (includes influencers).
- **Your own outreach** (to clinic owners) must be Spam-Act compliant: inferred consent only, a working unsubscribe on emails, opt-outs actioned within 5 business days.
- Before any live patient campaign: **Australian regulatory sign-off on templates.** Budget for it.
