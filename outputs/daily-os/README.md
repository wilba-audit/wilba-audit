## ☀️ The one command
Every morning, type **`/daily`**. In ~10 minutes I hand you:
- who to follow up + today's **~20 outreach messages** (written, warm + med spa),
- **fresh prospects** (I research them so your list never runs dry),
- **one social post** (script + shot list + b-roll + caption, ready to film),
- and today's **time-blocked schedule** below.

You just execute.

## 🥊 The 5-Hour Battle Rhythm

| Block | Time | What you do | Powered by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 · Prime** | 10 min | Run `/daily`. Everything gets generated. Skim it. | `/daily` |
| **1 · Send** 💰 | 75 min | Fire the warm + med-spa messages. Log each send. | message bank + scoreboard |
| **2 · Prospect + reply** | 60 min | Work replies, book calls. I top up your list. | `/prospects`, pipeline |
| **3 · Calls / follow-through** | 60 min | Take discovery calls when booked; else send proposals + warm nurture. | call script |
| **4 · Content** 🎨 | 60 min | Film the one post on your phone → post. (B-roll + caption already made.) | `/content`, social engine |
| **5 · Close-out** | 15 min | Update the scoreboard. Celebrate the day's wins. Prime tomorrow. | scoreboard |

**≈ 4.5–5 hours.** Cash blocks (0–2) are sacred. If the day gets cut short, keep those and drop content.

## 📏 The rules (stick to these)
1. **Outreach before content.** Cash before compounding. Always.
2. **Sell the call, not the build.** The guarantee carries it; price comes after the call.
3. **One niche.** Med spa (+ warm network). No new pies during the sprint.
4. **Log every send.** You control touches, not outcomes — so track touches.
