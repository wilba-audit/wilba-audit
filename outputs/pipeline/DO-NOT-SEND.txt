# was sent, because the send trigger was off, but a hold list that can empty without anyone
# noticing is the wrong shape for the job. This file is the sourcing side's authoritative answer

# from it.

# grade does not carry - row 181 is the live example, held because its address may be a decoded
# Cloudflare placeholder recorded as a published one. Those are called out in the row's notes and
# in the sourcing log, not here. A grade is a qualification verdict, not a send clearance.
