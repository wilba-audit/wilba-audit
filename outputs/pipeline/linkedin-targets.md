# LinkedIn targets — the 16 best clinics we cannot email

These are the strongest prospects in `near-misses.md`: clinics that clear every
part of WILBA's ideal-customer profile — several locations, a real named clinical
team, and a membership that banks money patients have already paid — but publish
no email address we can reach. LinkedIn is the only door left.

**How to use it.** Work top to bottom. Open the person's profile, connect, and
when they accept, open with the fact quoted in their section. The fact is there to
prove you have actually looked at their practice, so use their own words. Each
section also says how many locations they run and why email can't reach them, so
you know what you are walking into. Where no profile could be found the entry is
still here, with what we do know, so nobody re-researches it.
