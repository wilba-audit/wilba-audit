## 4. Historical precedent: how fast GTA V and RDR2 cheats were found and confirmed, where they surfaced, and how RDR2 cheats worked

### Takeaway
GTA V's cheats leaked the day *before* launch (Sep 2013), via a Reddit user, which suggests early retail copies were the source. RDR2's first cheats were being collected on fan forums on launch day (26 Oct 2018), roughly half were found within days, more emerged by mid-November, and the full set of 37 was found later. RDR2 hid its cheat phrases in the game world (newspapers, ledgers) and entered them in the Settings menu. Using a cheat disabled saving. If GTA 6 follows the RDR2 model, community discovery could take weeks rather than hours.

### Cited Findings
- **GTA V (2013):** a Latin Times article reports "18 cheat codes and many achievements have been leaked online", credits Reddit user "highpixels", and states "GTA V won't release until tomorrow". GTA V launched 17 Sep 2013, which dates this to about 16 Sep 2013 (inferred from the wording; the article date was not visible). — [Latin Times](https://www.latintimes.com/gta-5-release-date-news-18-cheat-codes-and-many-achievements-have-been-leaked-online-new-game-131194)
- **GTA IV precedent:** cheats also leaked before launch. — [TechCrunch, "Cheats for GTA IV leaked"](https://techcrunch.com/?p=25455)
- GTA V's full list includes console button inputs, in-game phone numbers and PC codes. — [Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/au-en/gta-5-cheat-codes)
- **RDR2 (launch 26 Oct 2018):** an RDR2.org forum thread, "Cheat Codes are out", started 26 Oct 2018. Within days players had found a few codes, about half of a suspected 33. — [RDR2.org Forums](https://www.rdr2.org/forums/topic/1139-cheat-codes-are-out/); [Clan of Idiots forum aggregation](https://www.clanofidiots.com/forums/forum/announcements/game-news-feeds/219945-red-dead-redemption-2-cheats-all-codes-we-ve-found-so-far)
- More RDR2 codes were reported on 15 Nov 2018, some needing specific newspapers or ledgers. "Greed is now a virtue" gives $500, and the max-Honor code requires Blackwater Ledger No. 67. — [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/red-dead-redemption-2-cheat-codes-rockstar-games/)
- ScreenRant later reported all 37 cheats found. — [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/red-dead-redemption-2-cheats-found/)
- **How RDR2 cheats work:** phrases are typed into the Settings, Cheats menu. Many unlock only after buying a specific newspaper at a certain story point, and newspapers are read under Satchel, Documents, Newspapers. Using any cheat disables saving. Counts conflict: 33 in early guides versus 37 later. One list says only 8 codes are newspaper-locked. — [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/red-dead-2s-cheat-codes-how-to-find-and-activate-t/1100-6462798/); [Push Square, Nov 2018](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2018/11/guide_red_dead_redemption_2_cheats_-_how_to_unlock_cheat_codes_and_what_they_do); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/red-dead-redemption-2-cheats/); [gta-zone.com](https://gta-zone.com/news/rdr2-cheat-codes/)
- A new RDR2 secret was still being discovered about seven years after launch, a sign of the long tail of in-game discovery. — [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Seven-years-later-a-player-discovers-a-new-secret-in-Red-Dead-Redemption-2.1141568.0.html)

### Inferences
- The first-surfacing channels historically are Reddit and fan forums (from early copies or datamining), then major-outlet guides (GameSpot, GamesRadar, Push Square, IGN) within days. YouTube "tested" videos typically follow.
- If GTA 6 uses RDR2-style in-world unlocks (phrases found in newspapers, phone contacts or similar), early claims will be partial. Claims of a "full list" in the first days should be treated as suspect.
- The pre-launch leak pattern (GTA IV, GTA V) means 18–19 Nov is the highest-risk window for real-but-unverified leaks mixed with fakes. Each needs on-console testing before being marked Confirmed.
